# μForge.io is:
- ***micro***-Forge.io: microelectronics, microcontroller, micro-power, micro-innovation ...
- ***you***-Forge.io: we work with ***you*** to bring your ideas to reality ...
---

**μForge.io** is an open platform for embedded systems and microelectronics development. Discover chips, development boards, reference designs, tutorials, examples, and reusable project recipes—all in one place. Build faster, learn continuously, and turn ideas into products with the support of an open developer community.

We are proud to partner with SiFli Technologies to bring the innovative SF32 family of microcontrollers to the open-source community, through accessible documentation, reference designs, software examples, and community-driven learning resources.

Whether you're building wearables, Bluetooth devices, audio products, or IoT solutions, **μForge.io** provides the tools, knowledge, and ecosystem to help transform ideas into products.
